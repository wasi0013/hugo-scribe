Scribe is a Hugo theme for people who write. It pairs a handwritten body font
with monospace details, keeps the page narrow enough to read comfortably, and
then gets out of the way.

## What you get

- **Three reading themes.** Light, sepia, and dark. First-time visitors get
  the one that matches their system; after that the choice is theirs.
- **A font toggle.** Handwriting is charming, but not for everyone. Readers
  can switch to a clean sans-serif from the gear menu, and the site remembers.
- **Fast pages.** One inlined stylesheet, one tiny script, no framework.
- **Sharing previews.** Open Graph and Twitter Card tags are filled in from
  each post. The cover above comes from this post's `featured_image`, which
  doubles as its sharing image.
- **The basics, done properly.** Tags, categories, RSS, pagination, syntax
  highlighting, and a 404 page.
