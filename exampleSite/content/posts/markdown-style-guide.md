This post exists to show how ordinary Markdown looks in Scribe. Paragraphs are
set at a generous size with plenty of line height, because the handwritten
face needs room to breathe.

Text can be **bold**, *italic*, or ~~struck through~~. Links pick up the
[accent colour](https://gohugo.io) of the active theme, and `inline code`
gets a quiet background of its own.

## Headings

Headings use the same face as the body, only heavier. Second-level headings
divide a post into sections.

### Third-level headings

These mark out subsections.

#### Fourth-level headings

And these are for the rare occasion you need to go deeper.

## Lists

Unordered lists are good for loose collections:

- A fountain pen
- A bottle of ink
  - Blue-black, for everyday notes
  - Red, for corrections
- A notebook that lies flat

Ordered lists are good for steps:

1. Write the first draft quickly.
2. Leave it alone for a day.
3. Cut everything that doesn't earn its place.
